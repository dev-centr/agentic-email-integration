= Why integration, not disposable inboxes
:navtitle: Integration vs disposable
:description: Why agentic email should attach to your owned mailbox instead of minting AgentMail-style disposable inboxes.

== The disposable inbox pitch

Some products give each agent a throwaway address. That looks tidy in demos. It fails for developer work:

* Vendor mail (GitHub, CI, registries) already lands in **your** identity.
* OAuth and app connections are tied to that identity.
* Threads, CC lists, and legal records need a stable owner.
* You cannot “own” a conversation that never touched your account.

== Integration model

`aei` watches **your** Gmail. Agents use the same CLI you trust (Himalaya). Drafts stay in your Drafts folder. Sending remains a human action until you opt into something riskier.

That is the whole product thesis: **agentic behavior without surrendering the mailbox**.
